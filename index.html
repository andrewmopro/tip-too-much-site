Tip Too Much

A private finance dashboard for one household. It gathers our own bank and card transactions in one place so we can see where the money goes.

This is not a public product. Tip Too Much is a personal project run on a home computer and used only by the members of one household. There is no sign-up, and it is not offered to anyone else.

What it does

  • Brings transactions from our checking, savings and credit card accounts into a single list.
  • Sorts spending into categories and shows monthly totals and trends.
  • Spots recurring charges and subscriptions.
  • Tracks which credit card statement credits we have used.

Privacy policy

Effective November 14, 2026. This policy covers all data the app handles. The sections below describe what is collected, how it is used and stored, how long it is kept, and how to remove it.

What data it accesses

Accounts are connected through Plaid. The app requests read-only access to:

  • Account names, types and the last four digits of account numbers.
  • Transaction history: date, merchant, amount and category.

It cannot move money, make payments or change anything at a bank. Bank usernames and passwords are entered with the bank or Plaid directly and are never seen or stored by the app.

How the data is used and stored

  • Transactions are used only to display the dashboard to the account holders themselves.
  • All data is stored in a database on a computer in our home. It is not uploaded to any cloud service.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The dashboard is reachable only from our private home network, not from the public internet.
  • Plaid credentials for the app are kept on that same computer and nowhere else.

Retention and deletion

  • Transaction history is kept for as long as the household uses the app, since long-term history is its purpose.
  • A connected account can be disconnected from inside the app at any time. This stops further syncing and removes the app's access to that bank.
  • Access can also be revoked through the bank's own connected-apps settings or at my.plaid.com.
  • An account holder can ask for their stored data to be deleted; it is removed within 30 days.
  • If the app is retired, all stored data is erased and every bank connection is removed.
